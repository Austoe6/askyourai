What only we answer

What is this service?

A private Telegram channel for members. You get accurate trade signals there. Membership is weekly. When payment stops, access stops.

What does a signal look like?

Three alerts, in order. The first tells you to prepare. The second tells you to stand by. The third is the entry signal. The first two exist so you are at the chart for the third.

What do I do with the entry signal?

You decide whether to trade it. We do not place the order for you. If you are unsure which button does what, ask your AI and name the platform you trade on.

How do I join?

Open the Telegram bot from signals2.com, pay the weekly Stripe checkout, and use the one-time invite the bot sends. There is no public channel link.

What if I cancel or the card fails?

You keep the week you already paid. After that, or after a short grace period if a payment fails, you are removed from the channel. A new payment gets a new invite.

Can someone else use my invite?

No. The invite works once and expires quickly. A join without an active subscription is removed.

Will you help me set up Telegram, MT4, or my broker?

No. That is a question for your AI. Tell it your phone type or your broker’s platform. We only speak for the signal and the membership.
